# Accuracy vs. Fairness in Philadelphia Property Assessments

## Group

5420 Group. Ssaumya, Tanmay.

## Changes since the proposal
We switched to log-price OLS and grouped CV by property to prevent repeat-sale leakage. Comparing OLS with the City on the same sales makes their errors comparable. We added a simulation to check misleading patterns caused by price binning. City assessments are excluded from predictors.

## Data
Loaded 583,716 properties, 524,331 transfer records, and annual assessments. Parcel matching retained 49,468 likely market sales from 2022-2024. Among single-property deeds, 81,510/81,617 with nonblank keys matched (99.87%); coverage including missing keys was 88.40%.

We retained regular single-property deeds and current single-family properties. We dropped unmatched keys, prices at or below $1,000, discrepancy flags, nonzero other consideration, missing transaction dates, and construction years after sale. The output is sale price in dollars; there are 10 original inputs before encoding.

## Split
Training: 34,127 sales from 2022-2023. Held-out 2024: 15,341; 15,254 have usable City assessments. Five training folds group repeat sales by parcel. Imputation, scaling, encoding, and retransformation are fitted within each fold. Seed: 5420. The 2024 outcomes were held out until final evaluation; no test-based retuning.

## Baseline
OLS predicts log price using livable area, lot area, age, bedrooms, bathrooms, stories, interior/exterior condition, quality, and ZIP. Areas receive log1p transforms. Predictions are converted back to dollars using a correction estimated only from the training data. OLS has no tuning parameter, so the grid is not applicable; five-fold CV validates the fixed specification.

## Result
Grouped-CV RMSE across 34,127 training-period sales was $116,769: the square root of the average squared dollar error, using one held-out-fold prediction per sale. This is not the 2024 test score or a total error across all sales.

On the same 15,254 test sales, OLS RMSE was $161,352 (95% CI: $126,945-$206,682); City RMSE was $159,866 ($130,485-$201,526). OLS MAE was $77,343; City MAE was $79,854. Intervals use 2,000 paired parcel-bootstrap resamples, keeping repeat sales together and predictions fixed.

The simulation used a known price function plus noise. We calculated predicted price / actual sale price. Even with the known function as the predictor, average ratios declined across actual-price bins. Grouping by predicted price largely removed this artificial decline. baseline.png shows only the OLS-versus-City error comparison.

## Issues found
Property features and classifications reflect 2026 records and may differ from those at the time of sale. Historical assessments may also include later corrections. We excluded room count because it was missing for about 94% of records. We screened out likely non-market transfers, but could not confirm that every remaining sale was an arm's-length transaction.
